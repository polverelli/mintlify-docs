## What is Lumx?

Welcome to Lumx, the seamless way to integrate stablecoin payments in Latin America.

Our APIs allow businesses to **collect, hold, convert, and pay out** using stablecoins or local currencies, with instant settlement and regulatory compliance built in.

Whether you're building global payment flows or managing enterprise treasuries, Lumx enables modern money movement.

## What you can build with Lumx

- **Send & receive payments**\
  Move money globally using local rails or stablecoins, with instant settlement.
- **Stablecoin accounts**\
  Create wallets to hold stablecoin balances securely.
- **Virtual accounts (soon)**\
  Issue USD deposit accounts for your users with local bank details.
